Your Specialty Care Referral

Spring Health

Your Provider Recommends Specialty Care

Your primary care provider has reviewed your treatment progress and believes that specialty mental health care could help you reach your goals more effectively.

This means you'll have access to specialized providers and treatment approaches tailored to your specific needs.

What Does This Mean?

Specialty care means working with a provider who has deep expertise in treating your specific condition. They can offer evidence-based approaches that are customized to your situation, and work alongside your primary care team to ensure coordinated care.

What Happens Next

1

Navigator Outreach (This Week)

A Spring Health Navigator will reach out to you by phone or email to introduce the specialty care option and answer any questions you have.

2

Schedule Initial Consultation (Next 1-2 Weeks)

If you're interested, the Navigator will help you schedule a consultation with a specialist who matches your needs and preferences.

3

Begin Specialty Treatment

You'll work with your specialist while maintaining continuity with your primary care provider. They'll coordinate to make sure your care is seamless.

This Is Your Choice

Your provider's recommendation is just that—a recommendation. You have full control over whether to proceed with specialty care. The Navigator will discuss this with you and respect whatever decision you make.

Common Questions

Will specialty care cost me more?

Specialty care is covered by your insurance plan. Your Navigator can discuss your specific coverage and any out-of-pocket costs before you commit.

Can I continue seeing my primary provider?

Yes! Specialty care works best as a collaborative effort. You'll continue working with your primary provider while also receiving specialized support.

What if I'm not ready yet?

That's completely okay. You can discuss this with your Navigator, and they can check back in with you at a later time if that feels better.

How do I prepare for the Navigator call?

Just think about your goals for treatment and any preferences you have (timing, format like virtual vs. in-person, etc.). The Navigator will guide the conversation from there.
